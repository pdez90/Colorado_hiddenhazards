      The Honorable Council
      of the City and County of Denver
      451 City and County Building.

                                                   Attention:   ZONING OOMMITTEE
      Gentlemen:

                We are attaching herewith for ;;rour consideration, a bill for an
      ordinance to amend Section 19, Article 'IV, of Ordinance No. 14, Series of
      1925, said section 19 being entitled "Front Yards in Residence Districts:
      A. Requirements", to read as follows:
                   1   (4)   In any residence district where one or more dwellings have
                             been erected on thru lots in a block having frontage between
                             two intersecting streets, the front yard space as established
                             by such dwelling or dwellings shall be the required front
                             yard space for dwellings hereafter erected in such block
                             fr ontage. 1

                The Board of Adjustment recommends this amendment to establish a
      rule whereby builders will be required to face houses in blocks containing
      thru lots, all in the same direction, affording uniform frontages and avoid-
      ing the possibility of one home owner having his front yard adjacent to his
      neighbor's rear yard.

                Trusting the Council will give this matter favorable and early co.n-
      sideration, we remain

                                                        Respectfully yours,




      cc Mayor Stapleton
         Mr. Harrington - Chairman Zonigg Committee
