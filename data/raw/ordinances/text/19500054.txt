SECTION l.        That sub-section A (l) of Section 19., 11 Front Yards in Residence
Districts" of Article IV, Ordinance No. 14, Series of 1925, as amended, be and
the same is hereby amended and re-enacted to read as follows:
     11   (1)       In a residence "A 11 district or a residence 11 B" district., the

     depth of the front yard between the front lot line and the front wall of
     a building shall be as follows:
     (a)           'J!Jhere all lots are unimproved in a block frontage between two
     intersecting streets, the depths of the front yards shall be 25 feet., but

     need not be more than 25% of the average or normal depth of the lots in
     the same block frontage, but in no case shall the required depths of the
    front yards be more than 35 feet.
     (b)           Where 20 per cent of a block frontage between two intersecting
     streets is improved with buildings having uniform front yard depths, and
    the remainder of such block frontage is unimproved, the required front
    yard depths of additional buildings shall extend back to the alignment of
    such existing buildings, unless such alignment is more than 35 feet.
    (c)            In a block frontage between two intersecting streets, where not
    more than four unimproved building sites are situate between lots improved

    with buildings having front yards of different depths, the required depth
    of front yards for a lot to be built upon shall be established at a point
    where the center line of such lot intersects a line drawn between the
