            FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING,
            AMENDING THE OFFICIAL MAP AND ESTAB-
            LISHING A ZONING CLASSIFICATION FOR
            A CERTAIN DESCRIBED AREA.
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
     SECTION 1.   The zoning classification of.the area in the
             '
 City and County of Denver included within the following bound-
 aries, said described area being a part of the area shown on
 that certain official map designated as S.E. 94, shall be and
 hereby is changed from R-1 to R-4:
     Commencing at the intersection of the center line of
     South Dahlia Street with the northerly line of the
     Colorado and Southern Railway right-of-way, which is
     the point of beginning; thence northerly along said
     center line of South Dahlia Street to its intersection
     with the center line extended of Asbury Avenue; thence we-
     sterly along said extended center line of Asbury Avenue
     to its intersection with the center line of the right-
     of-way of the Valley Highway; thence southeasterly along
     said center line of the right-of-way of the Valley High-
     way to its intersection with the northerly line of the
     Colorado and Southern Railway right-of-way; thence east-
     erly along said northerly line of the Colorado and
     Southern Railway right-of-way to the point of beginning,
and that certain official map designated as S.E. 94 shall be and
hereby is withdrawn and repealed and a map designated as S.E.
94-A, incorporating and reflecting the foregoing change in zon-
ing classification, shall be substituted in lieu thereof and
thereafter shall be a .part of the official map.
     SECTION 2.   The Council finds this ordinance is necessary
for the immediate preservation of the public health and public
safety and determines that it shall take effect immediately upon
its final passage and publication.
