                 FOR AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING AND
                 DIRECTING A SURVEY AND STUDY TO BE
                 MADE FOR THE PURPOSE OF SECURING DATA
                 FOR TEE REVISION OF THE ZONING
                 ORDINANCES AND ZONING MAP; AND APPROP-
                 RIATING $3,000 FOR THE EXPENSE OF SUCH
                 SUEVEY AND STUDY.


                 Whereas, a number of years have passed since the
     last general survey was made to secure information for the zon-
     ing of the City and County of Denver; and,
                 Whereas, during such period of time, the City has
    grown rapidly and there is need today for a thorough and compre-
    hensive study to be made for the purpose of revising and bringing
    up to date the zoning of the City and County of Denver;
                 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE
    CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
                 Section 1.   That the Board of Adjustment created by
    the zoning ordinances of the City and County of Denver be and
    said Board hereby is authorized and directed to proceed to make
    a comprehensive study and survey of conditions in the City and
    County of Denver for the collection of data upon whic            base
    any necessary revision of the zoning ordinances and zoning map.
    Said Board shall proceed with said study and survey with dili-
    gence and shall report its findings to the Council.
                 Section 2.   There is hereby appropriated, out of the
    1944 General Fund, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of Three
    Thousand Dollars to the fund of the City Council for the purpose
    of bearing the expense of such survey         and study, such expense
    to be paid out upon order of the President of the Council as
    other moneys in said City Council Fund are paid out.
