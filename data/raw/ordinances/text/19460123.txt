        We are joint owners of Lots 19 to 22, both inclusive, Block
24, and Lots 23 to 26, both inclusive, Block 25, University Park
Amended Map, in the City and County of Denver, and have owned the
same individually or jointly for approximately twenty years last
past.     The lots above described are located at the south end of
the 2000 block on South Milwaukee Street;      block 24 'b eing on the
east side of said street and block 25 being on the west side.
        The lots   above described in Block 24 are improved by a two-
story brick building, the basement and first floor of which are
used for a grocery and     meat market, and the second floor for two
living apartments.     The portion of said lots north and east of
the building is used for a driveway and parking space in connec-
tion with the market.     The lots above described in Block 25 are
wholly unimproved.
        All lots in said Blocks 24 and 25 fronting on Milwaukee
Street are now and at all times since a zoning plan was first
adopted for the City and County of Denver pursuant to Ordinance
have been zoned as business property.     We are advised that your
Honorable Board and Council are considering re-zoning said blocks
as residence B property.     We wish to protest such re-zoning
insofar as it affects the lots owned by us.     We purchased said
lots with the intention of using them for business purposes and
have have at all times been assessed and taxed as business prop-
erty.    We contemplate enlarging the business building on Lot ,s
19 to 22, Block 24, to occupy the entire frontage of those lots
on Milwaukee Street.     While we have at all times considered im-
