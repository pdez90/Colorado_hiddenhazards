   The purpose of this amendment to the present zoning ordinance
   is to prevent the over-crowding of land located in residence
   "B" districts by the erection of large multiple dwelling units,
   apartment houses and bungalow courts housing a large number
   of persons under one roof and within a single building unit.
   The Board is of the opinion that this type of development is
   detrimental and injurious to residence properties located in
   residence "B" districts and adjacent to residence "A" districts.
   Trusting that this proposed change in the present zoning
   ordinance will meet with the approval of the Council and that
