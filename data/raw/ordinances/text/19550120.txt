             FOR AN ORDINANCE ACCEPTING AND APPROVING
             THE PLAT OF SOUTHERN HILLS FILING NO. 'l'HREE.
     WHERE.AS, the owners of the following described land,
territory, or real property, to-wit;
    That part of the North One-half (N½) of the Southwest
    One-quarter (SW¼) of Section Thirty-six (Sec. 36),
    Township Four South (T. 4 S.), Range Sixty-eight West
    (R. 68 w.) of the Sixth Principal Meridian (6th P.M.)
    City and County of Denver, Colorado, described as
    follows: Beginning at a point on the West line of said
    SW¼ 35 feet South of the Northwest corner of said SW¼,
    said point being the Southwest corner of Southern
    Hills Filing No. Two; thence South along the West line
    of said SW¼ a distance of 1284.59 feet to the Southwest
    corner of the N½ of said SW¼; thence Easterly along
    the South line of the N½ of said SW¼ a distance of
    1066.34 feet; thence Northerly at an angle to the left
    of 90°00•38" a distance of 160.19 feet to a point of
    curve;. thence on a curve to the left having a radius
    of 313 feet an are distance of 174.81 feet to a point
    of tangent; thence Northwesterly on said tangent a dis•
    tance of 236.64 feet to a point of curve; thence on
    a curve to the right having a radius of 353 feet an
    are distance of 197.15 feet to a point of tangent;
    thence Northerly on said tangent a distance of 570.99
    feet, more or less, to the South line of Dartmouth Ave.
    as platted in Southern Hills Filing No. Two; thence
    Westerly along said South line, at an angle to the left
    of 90°, a distanee of 839.99 feet, more or less, to
    the point of beginning;
propose to lay out, plat, and subdivide said land, terri-
tory, or real property under the name and style or SOUTHERN
HILLS FILING NO. THREE, and have submitted to the Council of
the City and County of Denver a plat of such proposed sub-
division under the name and style aforesaid, showing the
adjacent streets and alleys and the topography of the
platted territory, and dedicating to the public the streets,

     avenues, boulevard and places thereon shown and not other-
     wise already dedicated for public use, also public utility
     easements as show, and
            'WHEREAS, said plat is accompanied by a certificate of
     title from the attorney for the City and County of Denver; and
            WHEREAS, said addition or subdivision was surveyed by
     or under the direction of the City Engineer of or for the
     City and County of Denver, and said Engineer has certified
     as to the accuracy of such survey and said plat or map and
     their conformity with the requirements of Article 342 of
     the Revised Municipal Code of the City and C01nty or Denver,
     and said plat has also been approved by the Zoning Administrator
     and the Planning Director;
     NOW, THEREFORE,
     BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
            Section 1.   That the Council hereby finds and determines
     that said land, territory, or real property has been platted
     in strict conformity with the requirements of the Charter
     of the City and County of Denver and the Revised Municipal
     Code of the City and County of Denver.
            Section 2.   That the said plat or map of SOUTHERN HILLS
     FILING NO. THREE and the dedication to the public of the
     streets, avenues, boulevard and places thereon shown and
     not otherwise already dedicated for public use, and also
     of public utility easements as shown be and the same are
     hereby accepted and approved by the Council of the City and
     County of Denver.
            Section 3.   The Council finds this Ordinance is nec-
     essary for the immediate preservation of the public health
     and public safety, and determines that it shall take effect
     immediately upon its final passage and publication.
