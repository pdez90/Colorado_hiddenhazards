                             A   BILL
                 FOR AN ORDINANCE CHANGING THE NAME
                 OF EDEN COURT WITHIN THE LIMITS OF
                 COLUMBINE ADDITION TO THOMPSON COURT.

            Whereas, in Columbine Addition recently laid out
there is a certain street described in the map of said Colum-
bine Addition as Eden Court; and,
            Whereas, it has been represented to the Council
that the name of said street should be changed to Thompson
Court in order to correspond more nearly with the name of an
existing street in the Addition immediately south of Columbine
Addition; now, therefore,
            BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND
COUNTY OF DENVER:
            Section 1.      That the name of the street lying be-
tween Blocks 2 and 3 in Columbine Addition and between 47th
and 48th Avenues be and the same is hereby changed from Eden
Court to Thompson Court.
            Section 2.      In the opinion of the Council this
ordinance is necessary for the immediate protection and pres-
ervation of the public health, safety, convenience and general
welfare, and it is enacted for that purpose, and shall be in
full force and effect immediately after its passage and final
publication.
