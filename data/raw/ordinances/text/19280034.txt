                         Gentlemen:

                                   A petition to amend the zone map has been filed
                         with the Board of Adjustment, signed by the owners of 65%
                         of the property located in a Business "A" Zone, said zone
                         extending along West Colfax Avenue from the alley East of
                         King Sto to the alley West of Wolff St. and adjacent to a
                         Commercial "A" Zone on the East and West and Residence "B"
                         Zone on the North and South; said petition requesting that
                         the Board recommend to the Council. that an ordinance be pre-
                         pared changing the classification-of the territory above
                         describe&, from a Business "A" Zone to a Business "On Zone •


                                  . This petition was considered by the Board at a
                         regular meeting held Tuesday, March 6, 1928, and after due
                         consider_ation of same and an inspection of the territory in
                         question by the members of the Board, a Resolution approving
                         the change and recommending that the Council pass an a,mend-
                         ing ordinance, was adopted. The Board recommends the change
                         for the following reaaons, towit:--

                                    (1) The territory in question is situated between
                         two commercial zones which are of~ lower classification than
                         ·the classification proposed by the petitioners.

                                   (2) The change from a Business "A" to a Business
                         "C" district, will not change in any way, the uses now allowed
                         in the district as provided in the use district regulations
                         of the zoning ordinance, except that apartment houses can be ·
                         erected within the boundary of the territory involved, should
                         the proposed change be madeo
