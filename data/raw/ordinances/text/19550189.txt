                        FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING,
                        ANiENDING TiiE OFFICIAL MAP AND CHANG-
                        ING THE ZONING CLASSIFICATION FOR A
                        CERTAIN DESCRIBED AREA.
     BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
            SECTION 1.      The zoning classification of the area in the
     City and County of Denver described as follows or included with-
     in the following boundaries, said described area being a part
     of the area shown on that certain official map designated as
     N.W. 19, shall be and hereby is changed from R-3 to B-2:
           Lot land South 10 1 of Lot 2, Block 1, Kennedy's
           Addition to the Town of Highland's.
     and that certain official map designated as N.w. 19 shall be and
     hereby is withdrawn and repealed and a map designated as N.W. 19-~
     incorporating and reflecting the foregoing change in zoning classi-
     fication, shall be substituted in lieu thereof and thereafter
     shall be a part of the official map.
            SECTION 2.      The Council finds this ordinance is necessary
     for the immediate preservation of the public health and public
     safety and determines that it shall take effect immediately upon
     its final passage and publication.
