SUBJECT:         Concerning the amendment of existing Ordinances
                 relative to vocal entertainment in Cabarets and
                 Motion Picture Theaters.

The accompanying Ordinance establishes s a class A Cabaret
or Night Club, a Cabaret offering vocal' entertainment but
does not offer dancing and vaudeville entertainment. The
license fee will be $50.00 per annum. The ordinance will
also allow personal appearances in motion picture theaters
not to exceed four persons in number offering vocal enter-
tainment only, without changing the classification of the
entertainment offered by the motion picture theater.
This matter was discussed at the Mayor-Council meeting
held the afternoon of April 25, 1950.
