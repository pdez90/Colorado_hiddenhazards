        Hon. City Council,
        Building.
        Gentlemen:
                  In connection with the proposed covering of irrigating
        ditches in Denver, we have carefully checked both the charter
        provisions and the state statutes; and do not find any provision,
        either of charter or stat~te, directly applicable to a public im-
        provement of this character.
                  We think, however, that the general powers of the Coun-
        cil are sufficient to enable the Council, itself, to prescribe
        a procedure. We have, therefore, prepared and hand you herewith
        for your consideration a bill for an ordinance creating a pro-
        cedure.
                   Under this bill it will be possible, in the case of
        any irrigating ditch, to provide that the cost of covering the
        same shall be divided, in such portions as the Council shall speci-
        fy, between:

                  First, the particular tracts of land which will be bene-
        fited by the covering of the ditch;
                          Second, the owners of the ditch, itself;
                  Third, the general taxpayers of the city, in those cases
        where a'part of the cost should be borne by the city as a whole.
                  Please understand that this suggested ordinance merely
        provides a form or method of procedure, leaving it open for de-
        termination in each particular case as to how much of the cost
        shall be placed upon the property owners, the ditch owners, and
        the general taxpayers of the city. This division of cost will, of
        course, have to be decided by appropriate separate ordinance in
        the case of each ditch to be covered.
