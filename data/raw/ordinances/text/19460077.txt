            and change the present Business "A" Zone along West 23rd Avenue at the intersections
            of Osceola, Perry and Quitman Streets, to a Residence "B" Zone, as shown on attached
            map.

                      Also attached is a petition signed by over one hundred owners of resident-
            ial. properties located in the illlnediate neighborhood of the present business district,
            requesting that this business mne be changed to residence, in order to prevent the
            further intrusion of business establishments and bungalow courts housing large nwn-
            bers of families.

                       The majority of the improvements in this neighborhood are single family
            dwellings, owned and maintained as private homes, and the Board is of the opinion
            that the .further encroachment of business or multiple dwel 1i ngs in the district will
            be veI7" detrimental and injurious to the present residential properties and will
            retard the future development of the district.

            thereto appeared and were heard.
