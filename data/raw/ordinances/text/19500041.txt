SUBJECT:            A Bill for an Ordinance Establishing Certain
                    Territory as Fire Zone No. 3


           The accompanying Ordinance establishes a
certain area as Fire Zone No. 3 (Masonry). The area
involved is that owned by the CB&Q Railroad Company and
which was recently annexed to the City of Denver. The
railroad desires to use the area for industrial purposes.
           This matter was discussed at the Mayor-Council
meeting held Tuesday afternoon, January 24, 1950.
