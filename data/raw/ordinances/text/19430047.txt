                                               Attention:   Zoning Committee

              Gentlemen:
              We are attachin~ herewith a bill for an ordinance to amend
              the zone map and change the present Business "An Zone on
              South Locust Street between Alameda Avenue and Cedar Avenue
              to a Residence "A" Zone and the present Business "A" Zone
              on South Holly Street between Alameda Avenue and a point
              100 feet south of Cedar Avenue to a Residence "A" and a
              Residence "B" Zone.
              These blocks are now vacant and are in an undeveloped section
              of the city and the Board of Adjustment recommends that they
              be changed to a higher classification, as the present business
              frontage is not needed.
              We trust that the Council will give this ~atter their favorable
              consideration and will enact this bill into an ordinance.
