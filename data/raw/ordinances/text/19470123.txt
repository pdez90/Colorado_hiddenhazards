                                                               Attention: Mr •. Ameter
                  Gemtlemen:
                                                      At your request, I today talked with
                  Father McCarthy, the new President of Regis College, relative
                  to the re-zoning of the West One Half of Block 17, East Berke--
                  ley. He received me very graciously. After stating my busi-
                  ness, he told me that he, of course, would have to take the
                  matter up with his advisors, and that he would let me know
                  within the next several days whether Regis College would or
                  would not object to re..:.zoning.
                          Father McCarthy frankly stated that the College had
                  determined not to do any building or renting of their land,
                  because they did not want to subject the entire acreage to
                  taxation. He further stated that at the present time, they
                  were interested in only two things: First: they wanted to
                  be sure that no small .buildings; disreputable in character,
                  or size, would go in the Block. I assured him that such was
                  the reason why I had. platted the Block into gO foot lots, at
                  the suggestion of Prouty Brothers. I also told him that the
                  price of the lots was such that no little holi-in-the-wall
                  outfit could afford to buy. I further told him that if he
                  wished to suggest this to the Zoning Board, I ~ould put into
                  a Deed or Deeds to the Federal lots, a restriction as to the
                  price or cost of buildings to be erected thereon.
                         Second: Father McCarthy wanted to be sure that
                 liquor was not disposed of on the premises. I told him that
                 I thought that the 500 foot Rule applied not only to build-
                 ings with schools, but also to the land of such schools.
                 However, if it did not, and he wished to suggest this to the
                 Zoning Board, I woul4, at the request of the Zoning Board
                 to me, put such restrictions in my Deeds.
                          Father McCarthy said that under no circumstances
                  did Regis College want to have any part in the re-zoning, and
                  that if they did have objections to re-zoning my side, he
                  would let me know within the next several days.
