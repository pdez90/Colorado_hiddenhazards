                 FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING,
                 AMENDING THE OFFICIAL MAP AND CHANG-
                 ING THE ZONING CLASSIFICATION FOR A
                 CERTAIN DESCRIBED AREA.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
      SECTION 1,    The zoning classification of the area in the
City and County of Denver described as follows or included within
the following boundaries shall be and hereby is changed from R-3
to B-4:
                 Lot 2, Block 13, Highland Park
and that certain official map designated as N.W. 18-A shall be and
hereby is withdrawn and repealed and a map designated as N.W. 18-B,
incorporating and reflecting the foregoing change in zoning classi-
fication but otherwise similar to and in all other respects main-
taining all matters and things shown on that map designated as
N,W, 18-A, shall be substituted in lieu thereof and thereafter
shall be a part of the official map.
      SECTION 2,   The Council finds this ordinance is necessary for
the immediate preservation of the public health and public safety
                                         '
