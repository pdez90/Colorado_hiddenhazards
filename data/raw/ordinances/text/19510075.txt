SUBJECT:     An Ordinance Concerning Filling Stations:
             Curbing, Set-Back of Pumps and Floor Height
             in Buildings.

The attached Ordinance clarifies and changes the set-back
distance of filling station pumps from property lines, pro-
 vides for a 6n x b" curb. along the property lines, which
has been customary in the past, and, requires filling
station floors to be not less than the same height of the
filling station pump island.
This Ordinance was requested by Traffic Engineering and the
Building Department, and, has been approved by the Zoning
Department and the Fire Prevention Bureau.
This matter was discussed at Mayor-Council meeting April 3,
1951.
