             FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING;
             Al\/tENDING THE OFFICIAL MAP AND ESTAB-
             LISHING A ZONING CLASSIFICATION FOR
             A CERTAIN DESCRIBED AREA.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
      SECTION 1.   The zoning classification of the area in the
City and County of Denver described as follows or included with-
in the following boundaries, said described area being a part
of the area shown on that certain official map designated as
S.E. 67, shall be and hereby is changed from R-2 to P~l:
          The S~ of Lot 9, Lots 10 to 14 inclusive,
          35; 36 and 39 to 48 inclusive, Block 7
          and Lots l to 4 inclusive, Block 6, all in
          Sherman Subdivision.
and that certain official map designated as S.E. 67 shall be and
hereby is withdrawn and repealed and a map designated as S.E.
67-A, incorporating and reflecting the foregoing change in zoning
classification, shall be substituted in lieu thereof and
thereafter shall be a part of the official map.
     SECTION 2.    The Council finds this ordinance is necessary
for the immediate .. preservation of the public heal th and public
safety and determines that it shall take effect immediately upon
its final passage and publication.
