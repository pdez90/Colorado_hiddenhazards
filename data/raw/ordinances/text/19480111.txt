                PETITION   OF   PROTEST


TO THE HONORABLE MEMBERS OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF DENVER, COLORADO:


  We, the undersigned, being residents now living in the
vicinity of the area proposed to be rezoned .:f'?-om a Residence"A"
Zone to a Residence "c" Zone by Councilman's Bill N0.106 and
Councilman's Bill N0.107, now pending before your honorable
body do hereby protest against said rezoning as contemplated in
each of said bills for the following reasons:
   The proposed rezoning is allegedly for the purpose of
providing additional space for the erection of more sanatarium
buildings without any evidence of the necessity therefor;     that
the Spears sanatarium organization has an existing permit to
erect additional buildings in the area of two square blocks
between 9th and 10th Avenues and Ivanhoe and Jersey Streets;
that a new building is now nearing completion; that the new
building together with the permit to erect additional buildings
in the aeea hereinabove mentioned will provide for reason-
able expansion should same prove to be necessary;
     That the proposed rezoning of the area described in
the afore-mentioned bills is unnecessary, uncalled for and
unwarranted and will result in unnecessary depreciation
of property values in the vicinity surrounding the proposed
areas;
     It is therefore respect.fully requested that    the proposed
ordinances designated by Councilman's Bill N0.106 and Councilman's
Bill N0.107 be rejected.
