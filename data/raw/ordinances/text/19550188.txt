             FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING,
             AlViENDING THE OFFICIAL MAP AND CHANG-
             ING THE ZONING CLASSIFICATION FOR A
             CERTAIN DESCRIBED AREA •.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
     SECTION l,   The zoning classification of the area in the
City and County of Denver described as follows or included within
the following bo~ndaries, said described area being a part of the
area shown on that certain official map designated as S.E. 72,
shall be and hereby is changed from R-1 to B-1:
     West 101.33 feet of the South 300 feet of Block 26,
     Cherry Creek Gardens.
and that certain official map designated as S.E. 72 shall be and
hereby is withdrawn and repealed and a map designated as S.E, 72-A,
incorporating and reflecting the foregoing change in zoning classi-
fication, shall be substituted in lieu thereof and thereafter
shali be a part of the official map.
     SECTION 2.   The Council finds this ordinance is necessary
for the immediate preservation of the public health and public
safety and determines that it shall take effect immediately upon
its final passage and publication.
