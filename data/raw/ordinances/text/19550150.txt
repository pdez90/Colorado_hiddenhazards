   • 1.     DECLARATION OF FUBLIC t-'JLICY.     For tte purpose of establishing and

maintaining sound, stable and desirable develo::iment within the territorial limits

of the municipality, this ordinance, and as here ased the term ordinance shall

be deemed to include the official map, shall not be amended except to correct

a manifest error in the ordinance or, because of changed or changing conditions

in a particular area or in the municipality generally, to re-zone an area or

extend the boundary of an existing district, or to change the regulations and

restrictions thereof, only as reasonably necessary to the promotion of the

public health, safety or general welfare.            Subject to the limitations of the

foregoing Declaration of Public Policy, an amendment to this ordinance may

be initiated by Council on its own motion or, in the manner and pursuant to

the procedure hereinafter set forth, may be initiated by any person, firm or

corporation filing an application therefor with the Depg.rtment of Zoning

Administration •

   • 2.     LIMITATIONS ON ALL PROPOSED AMENDMENTS.       All proposed amendments to

this ordinance, regardless of how or by whom initiated, shall be subject to
the following limitations:

   .2-1.     ADMINISTRATIVE EXAMINATION.       No amendment to this ordinance shall be

adopted until the amendment has been examined by the administrative agencies

as hereinafter set forth and the recommendations of those agencies obtained •

   • 2-2.    UNIFORMITY OF DISTRICT REGULATIONS AND RESTRICTIONS.        No amendment

to this ordinance shall be adopted whereby the regulations and restrictions

established-thereby are not uniform for each district having the same classifi-

cation and bearing the same symbol or designation on the official map, nor

      • 2-3.      MINIMUM SIZE OF NEW DISTRICTS.     No amendment to this ordinance shall

 be adopted whereby the zoning classification of an area is changed unless the

 area meets the following requirements as to minimum size:

      .2-3(1).       A proposed R-0 or R-~ District shall be large enough in gross area
 so that at least one-half of the number of existing building sites included in

 the proposed district are entirely within the interior of the district and do

 not abut a Business or Industrial District or an R-2, R-3 or R-4 District.            For

 the ourooses of this section,         all vacant land included in the proposed district

 shall be divided into minimum size Zone Lots permitted in an R-0 or R-1 District

 for structures designed or used, either wholly or partly, for residential occu-

oancy;

      .2-3(2).      A proposed B-1 or B-2 District shall contain at least 70,000

 square feet of gross area; provided, however, that a proposed B-1 District

which would abut an existing B-2, B-3, B-4, B-5, I-1 or I-2 District shall be

exempt from any requirement of minimum size;

      .2-3(3).      A proposed R-2, R-3, R-4, B-3, B-4, B-5, I-0, I-1 or I-2 District

shall contain at least eight acres of gross area; provided, however, that a pro-

nosed I-0 District which would abut an existing I-1 or I-2 District shall be

exempt from any requirement of minimum size •

     • 2-4.      ABUTMENT OF INCOMPATIBLE DISTRICTS.     No amendment to this ordinance

shall be adopted whereby any boundary line of an R-3, R-4,B-5, I-1 or I-2 Dis-

trict would abut directly upon the boundary line of an existing R-0, R-1 or R-2

District unless the boundary line is coincidental with the center line of a

street, alley or other public property of comparable width •

     • 2-5.      ABUTMENT OF COMPATIBLE DISTRICTS.     No amendment to this ordinance sh~lJ

be adopted whereby a P-1 District zoning classification is established for .an
area unless the area abuts an existing R-4, B-1, B-2, B-3, B-h, B-5 I-1 or I-2

nance shall be adopted whereby a B-4 District zoning cJassification is estab-

lished for an area unless there is a clear and demonstrable necessity in the area

for those uses which are permitted in a B-4 District and are not permitted in
a B-3 District •

    •3. APPLICATIONS FOR AMENDMENTS •
    •3-1.    BY WHOM MADE.   Any person, firm or corporation may apply for an

amendment to this ordinance •

    •3-2.    FILING OF APPLICATIONS.     All applications for amendments to this

ordinance shall be filed in writing with the Department of Zoning Administration •

    •3-3.    CONTENTS OF APPLICATIONS.    Without in any way limiting the right to

file additional material, all applications for amendments to this ordinance
shall contain, at least, the following:

   .3-3(1).     The applicant's name, address and interest in the application

and the name, address and interest of every person, firm or corporation repre-

sented by the applicant in the application; provided, however, that if the

application is for the designation of an area as a B-2 or B-3 District it

shall contain the concurrence of the owner or owners of the entire land area

to be included within the proposed district, the owner or owners of all struc-

tures then existing thereon and all incumbrancers of said land area and struc-

tures and, additionally, shall contain sufficient evidence to establish that

the aoplicants are all the owners and incumbrancers of the designated land area

and structures, intend actually to develop the designated area and have both
the means and ability so to do;

    .3-3(2).   The nature, description and effect of the proposed amendment;

   .3-3(3).    If the proposed amendment would require a change in the official

map, a plat showing the land area which would be affected by the proposed

amendment, the present zoning classification of the area, the land area of all

abutting districts and the present zoning classification thereof, all public

and private rights-of-way and easements bounding and intersecting the desig-

nated area and the abuttinis districts; provided, however, that if the proposed

amendment would designate an area as a B-2 or B-3 District, the plat shall

show, additionally, the location of all existing and proposed structures with

suppo~ting open facilities, the specific ground area, herein called the Zone

Lot, to be provided and continuously maintained for the structure or struc-

tures containing a Use or Uses by Right and, if the irregularity of grade

exceeds ten feet in elevation, the topography of the ground in the proposed

district in contour intervals not exceeding two feet;

   .3-3(4).    The time schedule, if applicant has such a time schedule, for

the beginning and completion of development planned by the applicant in the

applicable area.    If the development is planned in stages, the time schedule

shall indicate the successive stages and the development planned for each

stage;

   .3-3(5).    If the proposed amendment would require a change in the official

map by rezoning an area to a type of district not already established in this

ordinance at the time the amendment is proposed, the applicant shall furnish

all regulations and restrictions for the proposed district;

   .3-3(6).    If the oroposed amendment would require a change in the official

map by rezoning an area from an existing Residential District to a free-

standing Business District, would more than double the area of an existing

Business District entirely surrounded by one or more Residential Districts or

would enlarge the area of an existing Business District by more than eight

acres, the applicant shall furnish a written description of the market area

intended to be served by the development, the population thereof, the effec-

tive demand for the proposed facilities and any other information describing

the relationship of the proposed develonment to the needs of the applicable

area;

   .J-3(7).    The error in this ordinance that would be corrected by the pro-
posed amendment;

   .4.     EXAMINATION OF APPLICATIONS •
   . 4-1. AD.MINISTRATIVE EXAMINATION. Upon receipt of an application for an
amendment, properly and completely made as hereinabove set forth, the Depart-

ment of Zoning Administration shall transmit copies of the application to the

Planning Office and to any other agencies, either public or private, which

might be affected by the amendment.        If considered necessary, any such agency

may require the applicant to furnish additional information of a pertinent

and reasonable nature.     Any such agency may transmit comments and recommenda-

tions concerning the application to the Department of Zoning Administration •

   •5.     DISPOSITION OF APPLICATIONS •

   . 5-1. ADMINISTRATIVE DISPOSITION. When an application for an amendment,
including agency comments and recommendations thereon, is completely assembled,

the Department of Zoning Administration shall submit to Council such com~

pleted application aµd ag~ncy reports •

   •5-2.     LEGISLATIVE DISPOSITION.   Council shall examine all applications

and agency reports submitted to it and shall direct the Department of Zoning

Administration as to further action thereon.
