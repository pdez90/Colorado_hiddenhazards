SUBJECT:             An Ordinance giving oomprehensive and inte-
                     grated treatment to the subj eot of "Permanent
                     Occupancy of Public Propertyn, which Ordinance
                     will form an entire chapter in the forthcoming
                     Municipal Code in the Main Division entitled
                     Public Ways and Places.


          The accompanying Ordinance wi   repeal certain
parts of ordinances, certain entire ordinances, a chapter
of the present Building Gode, and various sections of the
Municipal Code of 1927. In respect to style and presen-
tation, it is almost a complete revision of all these
materials and gives, for the first time, a comprehensive
and internally consistent coverage of this important area.
In respect to substance there are no material changes.
Several rather inconsequential alterations have been made,
for example, the Manager of Improvements and Parks and not
the Art Commission is given the power to approve or dis-
approve of sidewalk clocks. The emergency notice sections
in the paragraphs on vaults under sidewalks are changed
from a stipulated number of days period to a reasonable
period to be determined by the extent of the hazard. Other
rather insignificant changes in substance have been made to
bring certain paragraphs into conformity with Charter re-
quirements.

          This matter was discussed at the Mayor-Council
meeting on Tuesday, January 10, 1950.
