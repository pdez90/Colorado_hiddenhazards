             Gentlemen:

                       We are submitting herewith a bill for an ordinance to amend Ordinance
             No. 14, Series of 1925., by ma.king the following changes in the Zone Map.

                       The Board is recommending that the two half blocks fronting on Oneida
             Street between 21st Avenue and 22nd Avenue, which is now zoned Business "A" be
             changed to a Residence 11A 11 and Residence "B" Zone, aaj that the two half blocks
             fronting on Oneida Street between 22nd Ave. and 23rd Ave. be changed from
             Residence 11 A11 to a Business "A" with buffer Residence "B" and "C" Zones surround-
             ing this proposed Business "A" Zone.

                       The present Business 111 11 Zone between 21st Ave. and 22nd Ave. is vac-
             ant, and the proposed Business "A" Zone is also vacant.

                       The lots in the proposed new Business 11Afl Zone are held in single owner-
             ship and the owner of these lots has submitted to the Board a block development
             plan for this proposed Business 11A" Zone which has been approved by the Board.
             The deYelopment of this block by a single owner will be much ioore desirable than
             if the block were improved with business buildings erected by several different
             owners, as a uniform architectural design can be carried out if the entire block
             is developed by one owner.

                          The Board therefore recommends that this change in Zone Classification
            be made.
