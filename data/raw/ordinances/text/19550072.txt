     Dear Mr. Holland:
          Upon examination of the bill concerning absentee
     voting, it was thought that the proposal did not speci-
     fically include the voting on bond questions and that
     an additional provision should be included in the bill
     for this purpose.
          I am accordingly submitting herewith a number of
     copies of an amendment to Section 2 which now provides
     that upon the application for an absentee ballot where
     bond questions are being voted upon, the Election Com-                       •
     mission shall submit separate ballots, separate instruc-
     tions, and separate return envelopes. The affidavits
     appearing on the return envelopes are different in that
     the envelope for the bond question contains the additional
     qualification that the voter shall be a taxpayer.
             The proposed amendment has been approved by the
     attorneys who are passing upon the legality of the
     present bond proposals and has also been approved by
     Mr. Joe Dinan, Secretary of the Election Commission.
          This entire Section 2 should be substituted for the
     present Section 2 of the bill.
