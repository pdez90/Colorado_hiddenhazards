                To:       The members of the Denver City Council.

                Gentlemen:

                      The Denver Board of Real tors feels that any legislation which creates
                hardships to a majority of the people for the benefit of a few is not in the
                interests of the people· of any community, and should not be enacted.

                      We further feel that any legislation which directly affects the value
                of property owned by people of a community should be enacted only after
                exhaustive study and in keeping with a general over-all program designed to
                enure to the lasting benefit or most of the people of the community.

                      The proposed off-street parking ordinance in our considered opinion
                would cause undue hardship and loss of value of property to a large number
                of the people of Denver. It is an ordinance which, by its nature, should be
                a part of the overall general re-zoning program of the City- and, as such,
                studied and presented as an integral part of that broad program, aid not as
                a separate piece of legislation.

                      The Board of Realtors feels strongly that further action by 6Enmcil on
                the proposed off-street parking ordinance should be deferred for further
                stud7 and analysis in conjunction with the development of the broad re-zoning
                program. The Board offers its assistance ands errices in making necessary
                and proper studiea and surveys to deve1op suitab1e and workable of't-stzeet
                parking regulations for incorporation into the general re-zoning program.

                Finally, the Board of Realtors respectfully expresses its opposition to this
                proposed ordinance as it is now written as being premature and too harsh in
                its provisions, and causing undue hardship to a great many Denver property
                owners.

                                                                                           Respectfully subnitted,
