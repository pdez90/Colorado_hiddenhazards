        FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING AND
        RESTRICTING THE ERECTION AND REMODELING
        OF SIGNS IN THE CIVIC CENTER AREA; AMEND-
        ING THE REVISED MUNICIPAL CODE IN RESPECT
        THERETO; AND REPEALING ORDINANCE NO. 179
        OF THE SERIES OF 1952.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
    Section 1.       That Section 622.7 of the Revised Municipal
Code, as amended, relating to the zoning of signs in the
Civic Center area be, and the same is, hereby amended and re-
enacted to read as follows:
            ~7 .   Signs Near Civic Center.     It shall be
    unlawful to erect or remodel any sign within the
     Civic Center area delineated by the heavy solid .
    line on the following map.·         It shall be unlawful
    to erect or remodel within one hundred fifty (150)
    feet of the delineated Civic Center area any sign
    which does not advertise or pertain wholly to a
    business conducted on the premises, or any roof
    sign, ground sign, projecting sign, or projecting
    pole sign.       The advertising area of flat or wall
    signs within such area may be of any shape but
    shall not exceed the product of the frontage of
    the premises in feet multiplied by three (3)
    provided, however, said advertising area in any
    event may be at least one hundred (100) square
    feet in area.       It shall be unlawful to erect or
