                  FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING, AMEND-
                  ING ARTICLE 611. (ADMINISTRATION AND ENFORCE-
                  MENT) OF CHAPTER 610. (ZONING) OF THE REVISED
                  MUNICIPAL CODE, AS AMENDED.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE ffiUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
       SECTION 1.          Section .4. (General Provisions for Administration
and Enforcement) of Article 611. (Administration and Enforcement)
of Chapter 610. (Zoning) of the Revised Municipal Code, as amended,
is hereby amended by adding thereto Sub-section .4-10. reading as
follows:
       11
            .4-10.    ZONE LOT FOB. EXISTING STRUCTURES AND USES. The land
area occupied by a use and/or the building site heretofore designa-
ted and occupied by each structure existing on the effective date
of this ordinance (February 11, 1955) shall, for the purposes of
this ordinance, be deemed the Zone Lot for such use and/or structur
and shall be continuously provided and maintained therefor in ac-
cordance with the provisions of this ordinance for uses and/or
structures in the district in which each such use and/or structure
is located.          Upon application to and approval by the Department of
Zoning Administration, the boundaries and area of a designated
Zone Lot may be amended if full compliance with all requirements of
this ordinance can be maintained."
       SECTION 2.          The Council finds this ordinance is necessary for
the immediate preservation of the public health and public safety
and determines that it shall take effect immediately upon its final
passage and publication.
