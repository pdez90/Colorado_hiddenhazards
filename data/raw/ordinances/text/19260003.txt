                                   January 6, 1925




           The Honorable Council
                  of the
           City and County of Denver.
           Gentlemen:
                     There is at the present time a business zone lo-
           cated along 29th Avenue from Downing Street east to Franklin
           Street; and also the frontage along 34th Avenue is now a
           Residence 11 C11 zone.
                     This Board has made a personal inspection of these
           two streets and believes that the business zone along 29th
           Avenue should be changed to a residence zone, as it is prac-
           tically all improved with residences, and there is very
           little vacant property available for business use. Thirty-
           fourth Avenue is a car line street, it is a logical business
           street and there are a number of corners vacant which would
           be available for business uses if the zoning of this street
           were changed from residence to business,
                         This Board therefore recor.m11ends to your Honorable
           Body that ordinances be prepared changing the zoning of the
           Business uB 11 zone along 29th Avenue and extending a distance
           of 100 feet each side of it from the alley east of Downing
           Street to the alley east of Franklin Street, to a Residence
           11
              C 11 zone. Also changing the Residence ncu zone a.long 34th
           Avenue and extending a distance of 100 feet each side of it
           from the alley east of Downing Street to the alley west of
           Franklin Street, to a :Business "B" zone.
                    Trusting that the Council may see fit to have these
           recommendations put into effect by the necessary ordinances,
           we are
                                            Very respectfully yours,
