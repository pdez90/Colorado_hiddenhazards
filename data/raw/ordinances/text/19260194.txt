              To the Honorable Council,
              The City and County of Denver,
              Building.

                     Gentlemen:
                          At a special meeting held by the Board of Adjustment Tuesday,

              July 27, 1926, the following resolution with reference to the petition

              of the owners of the First Addition to Lakota Heights, was adopted.

                         "Whereas: The owners of the First Addition to Lakota Heights,

             which addition has recently been approved and made a part of the City

             a.nd County of Denver by the Council of the City and County of Denver,

             have petitioned this Board that the East½ of Block 19 which includes

             lots 25 to 48 inclusive, be changed from a Residence "A" Zone to a

             Business nA" Zone,and

                         Whereas: The change from a Residence "A" Zone to a Business

             "A" Zone at this location would serve the publllc convenience and welfare,

             and eould not be detrimental to adjacent residential property, as said

             proposed Business Zone would front on Arapahoe County on the East and

            Wou.ld be adjacent to a Correctional Institution and would also be located

             on two main traffic streets viz: Arizona Ave., and South Celorado Blvd.,

             and

                         Whereas: There is now located a Business "A" Zone on South

             Colorado Blvd. between Tennessee Ave., and Mississippi Ave., which

             cannot be used for· business purposes on account of restrictions in the

             deeds.
