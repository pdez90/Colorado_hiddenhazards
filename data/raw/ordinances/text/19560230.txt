BE J:T ENACTED BY THE CDUNC!L OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:

      Section 1.       The zoning classification~ the area in the
City and County of Denver described as follows or included with-
in the following boundaries shall be and hereby is changed from
8-2 to I-O and the official map shall~and hereby is amended and

      public rights of way, but only to the center line
      thereof, which are immediately adjacent to the afore-
      said specifically described area.

for the immediate preservation of the public health and public
