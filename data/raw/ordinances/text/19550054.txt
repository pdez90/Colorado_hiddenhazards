                    FOR AN ORDINANCE ACCEPTING AND
                    APPROVING THE PLAT OF HARVEY
                    PARK ADDITION FILING NO. 13.
            WHEREAS, the owner of the following described land,
     territory, or real property, to-wit;
            A part of the North One-half (N½} of Section Thirty-
            one (Sec. 31), Township Four South (T.48.), Range Sixty-
            eight West (R.68W.) of the Sixth Principle Meridian
            (6th. P.M.), City and County of Denver, State of Colorado,
            more particularly described as follows:
            Beginning at the Northwest corner of said Section Thirty-
            one (Sec.31}; thence East along the North line of said
            Section Thirty-one (Sec.31), a distance of 3,274.21 feet;
            thence South parallel with the West line of said Section
            Thirty-one (Sec.31), a distance of 232.21 feet to a point
            of curve; thence Southerly alons a curve to the right
            having a radius of 830.00 feet (central angle 30°49'03",
            tangent distance 228.76 feet) and an arc distance of 446.43
            feet to a point of tangent; thence Southwesterly along
            said tangent 889.97 feet to a point of curve; thence
            Southwesterly along a curve to the left having a radius
            of 637.80 feet (central angle 30°49'03", tangent distance
            175.78 feet) and an arc distance of 343.05 feet to a point
            of tangent; thence South along said tangent and parallel
            withsaid West line of Section Thirty-one (See. 31) a dis-
            tance of 175.25 feet; thence West parallel with the North
            line of said Section Thirty-one (Sec.31) a distance of
            2611.00 feet to said West line of Section Thirty-one
            (Sec. 31); thence North 1930.00 feet to the point of
            beginning;
     proposes to lay out, plat, and subdivide said land, territory,
     or real property under the name and style of HARVEY PARK ADDITION
     FILING NO. 13, and has submitted to the Council of the City and
     County of Denver a plat of such proposed subdivision under the
     name and style aforesaid, showing the adjacent streets and alleys
     and the topography of the platted territory, and dedicating to
     the public the streets, avenues and other public places thereon
     shown and not otherwise already dedicated for public use, also
     public utility easements as shown; and
            WHEREAS, said plat is accompanied by a certificate of

title from the attorney for the City and County of Denver; and
       WHEREAS, said addition or subdivision was surveyed by or
under the direction of the City Engineer of or for the City and
County of Denver, and said Engineer has certified as to the
accuracy of such survey and said plat or map and their conf-
ormity with the requirements of Article 342 of the Revised
Municipal Code of the City and County of Denver, and said
plat has also been approved by the Zoning Administrator and
the Planning Director;
NOW, THEREFORE,
BE   IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
       Section 1.    That the Council hereby finds and determines
that said land, territory, or real property has been platted in
strict conformity with the requirements of the Charter of the
City and County of Denver and the Revised Municipal Code of the
City and County of Denver.
       Section 2.    That the said plat or map of HARVEY PARK
ADDITION FILING NO. 13 and the dedication to the public of the
streets, avenues and other public places thereon shown and not
otherwise already dedicated for public use, and also of public
utility easements as shown, be and the same are hereby accepted
and approved by the Council of the City and County of Denver.
       Section 3.    The Council finds this Ordinance is necessary
for the immediate preservation of the public health and public
safety, and determines that it shall take effect immediately
upon its final passage and publication.
