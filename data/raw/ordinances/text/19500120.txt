    BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF' DENVER:
              Section 1.    That the following described territory
    located in the fire district of the City and County of Denver
    and now zoned as Fire Zone No. 3 be and the same is hereby
    changed from Fire Zone No. 3 to a Fire Zone No. 4:
              Commencing at the point of intersection
              of Monaco Street Parkway extended and
              the center line of 35th Avenue extended,
              thence North to 38th Avenue extended;
              thence East to Quebec Street; thence
              South on Quebec Street to the center line
              of 35th Avenue extended; thence West to
              the point of beginning.
              Section 2.    That the Chief Building Inspector and
   the Chief of the Fire Department are hereby authorized and
   directed to amend• the official Fire District iVIap, dated
   May 14, 1948, as amended, on file in the Office of the City
    Clerk of the City and County of Denver, pursuant to the pro-
   visions of Ordinance No. 108, Series of 1948, to conform to
   the change specified in Section 1 hereof.
              Section J.    In the opinion of the Council this
    Ordinance is necessary for the immediate protection and pre-
    servation of the public health, safety, convenience, and
    general welfare, and it is enacted for that purpose and shall
   be in full force and ef.fect immediately after its passagr., and
   final publication.
