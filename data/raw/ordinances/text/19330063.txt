          FOR AN ORDINANCE REGULATING AND LICENSING
              THE RETAIL DISTRIBUTION OR SALE OF ICE
              FROM ICE DELIVERY STATIONS, AND PRO-
              VIDING PENALTIES FOR THE VIOLATION
              THEREOF.

                                ----------
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF TEE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:

          Section 1.      License.    Any person, firm, association,
or corporation, engaging in the business of distributing or
selling at retail ice from ice delivery stations, shall first
obtain a license therefor from the Manager of Saf'ety and
Excise.    The annual license fee shall be Ten Dollars ($10.00)
                                                  )'

per year, or unexpired portion thereof, for each and every
station, and shall be payable on or before the first day of
May 0£ each calendar year.


          Section 2.      Source S?.f._ SupplI·   Before obtaining a
license., as herein provided, every person, firm, association,
or corporation., engaging in the business of distributing or
selling ice at retail from ice delivery stations# shall file
with the Manager of Health and Charity a statement, sworn to
before a Notary Public, stating the source of the ice supplied.

   If, upon examination, the Manager of Health and Charity
   shall determine that the ice obtained from said source of
   supply is suitable for human consumption or use, he shall
   certify the fact to the Manager of Safety and Excise, who
   shall then issue the license.           No change in the source of
   ice supply shall be made without the written consent of
   the Manager of Health and Charity.

              Section 3.     Building.     All retail ice delivery
   stations shall be located in a district where such use is
   permitted in the Zoning Ordinance of the City and County of
   Denver as the same now exists, or as the same may be amended
   in the future.        The delivery station shall be located in a
   building situate upon a lot so as to provide ndrive-in"
   service.
              The building shall not be lower than Type III con-
   struction if in the Inner, Auxiliary or Middle Fire Districts;
   shall not be lower than Type IV construction with a veneer of
   masonry or cement stucco if located in the Outer Fire Districts,
   and shall not be lower than Type IY construction if located
   outside the Fire Districts.           The reference herein to types of
   construction and to Fire Districts is to be construed. as the
   same are now provided, or may hereafter be provided by
   ordinance.

              Section 4.    All ice sold from a retail ice station
   shall be sold by weight, and the scales upon which the same
   is weighed shall be sealed by the Inspector of Weights and
  Measures.

        Section 5.   Penalties.   Any person, firm,
association or corporation, or his, their, or its servants,
agents or employes who shall violate any of the provisions
of this ordinance,shall be subject to a fine of not less
than Ten Dollars ($10.00), nor more than Three Hundred
Dollars ($300.00) for each offense.

        Section 6.   The provisions of Section 3 of this
ordinance shall not be retroactive.

        Section 7.   In the opinion of the Council, this
ordinance is necessary for the immediate protection and
preservation of the public health, safety, convenience and
general welfare, and it is enacted for that purpose and shall
be in full force and effect immediately after its passage and
final publication.

        PASSED BY THE COUNCIL and signed by its President
