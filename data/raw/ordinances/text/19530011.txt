       Parkway and South Sheridan Boulevard.

       to this proposed zone change.

          SUBJECT     Rezoning i\l"E corner Alameda and South Sheridan.



                 1.   Location: Approximately one acre on NE corner of intersection; now
                      vacant. "Business A" zone lies across street South. Jefferson County
      .               R-2 zone lies West; Denver "Residence A" zone lies North •

                 2.   Rezoning Requested: At present, site is zoned 11 :tesidence A".   Requests
                      "Business A" zone to permit shopping development.

                 3. Pertinent Considerations:
                           A.   New residential subdivisions are developing in the neighborhood.

                           B.   Alameda is being protected as a parkway by Jefferson County, by
                                requiring all business buildings to be set back one hundred (100)
                                feet from right-of-way.

                           C.   Traffic volumes and speeds on Alameda are growing, and should be
                                protected from roadside parking friction.

                 4. Planning Office Com.,nents:
                           A.   The requested rezoning to 11:0usiness A" would not damage the
                                character of the district or the efficiency of Alameda parkway
                                provided:

                                  (1)   A 100 foot setback from the Alameda right-of-way should
                                        be maintained for any business structure.

                                  (2)   Complete off-street parking should be maintained on the
                                        11 i3usiness A" property.



                                  (3)   Entrances to the parking lot should not be permitted
                                        from Alameda. A suitable bumper or other barrier should
                                        be erected to prevent improper entrance from Alameda.

                                  (4) One curb cut on each side, Sheridan and Zenobia, should
                                        be sufficient. Cuts should be located away from the
                                        Ala..~eda corners.
