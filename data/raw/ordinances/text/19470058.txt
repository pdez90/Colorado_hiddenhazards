                                        BILL
                       FOR .AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE PLAT OF
                       "FRIEND'S RESUBDIVISION OF BLOCK 17 EAST
                       BERKELEY" IN THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER
                       AND ACCEPTING THE ALLEY THEREIN LAID OUT.
     BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER.
     WHEREAS, the owners of Block 17, East Berkeley in the City and County of
Denver, State of Colorado, have submitted to the Council a plat of a proposed
resubdivision of said block, which shows the alley and the topography of the
platted territory; and
     WHEREAS, said plat is accompanied by the proper certificates in
accordance with the requirements of the Charter and Ordinances of said City
and County, as shown upon said plat· so submitted.
     NOW, THEREFORE:
     Section 1:-That said Plat of "FRIEND'S RESUBDIVISION OF BLOCK 17 EAST
BERKELEY", in the City and County of Denver, 3nte of Colorado, be and the
same is hereby approved, and the alley dedicated and sho~m upon said plat is
hereby accepted by the City and County of Denver.
     Section 2:-In the opinion of the Council this ordinance is necessary for
the immediate protection and preservation of the public health, safety, con-
venience and welfare, and it is enacted for that purpose and shall be in full
~orce and effect immediately after its passage and final publication.
