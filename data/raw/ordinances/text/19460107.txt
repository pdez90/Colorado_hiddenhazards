       To the Honorable, The Council
       of the City and County of Denver,
       Room 451, City and County Building.

                                          Attention: ~omittee:

       Gentlemen:

                 We are attaching herewith for your consideration, a bill for an
       ordinance to amend Ordinance No. 14, Series of 1925, (the Zoning Ordinance) and
       to change the following described territory from a Residence "B" District to a
       Business "! 11 District:

                      The south 250 feet of Irene Park, a subdivision of Block 14, Maple-
                      wood Subdivision, said lots being bounded on the north by a line
                      distant 250 feet north of Jewell Avenue; on the east by South Jackson
                      Street; on the south by Jewell Avenue; and on the west by South
                      Garfield Street ..

                 We are also attaching herewith a petition signed by property owners
       living in the immediate neighborhood, requesting that the above described terri-
       tory be changed to a Business 11A11 District.
                 The Uni varsity of Denver has now tmder construction a large housing
       project for G. I. students attending the University, and this housing deelopment
       will be adjacent to and south of this proposed business zone, and will extend
       from South Monroe Street to South Colorado Blvd.

                 There is also a new residential development to the west of this proposed
       business district, and the nearest district now zoned for business and improved
       with store buildings is at least a mile distant from this rapidly developing
       residential neighborhood.

                 The Board of Adjustment recommends that this small Business "A" District
       be located north of Jewell Avenue between South Garfield Street and South Jackson
       Street as a neighborhood shopping center for the accoJlll1X)dation of the residents
