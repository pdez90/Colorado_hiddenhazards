                  FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING,
                  CHANGING THE ZONING CLASSIFICATION
                  FOR A CERTAIN DESCRIBED AREA.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
      Section 1.      The zoning classification of the area in the
City and County of Denver described as follows or included with-
in the following boundaries shall be and hereby is changed from
B-4 to B-6:
      Commencing at the intersection of the center line
      of 20th Street with the center line of the north-
      east-southwest alley between Welton Street and
      Glenarrn Place, which is the true point of begin-
      ning; .thence northeasterly along the center line
      of said alley to its intersection with the center
      line of 27th Street; thence northwesterly along
      the center line of 27th Street to its intersection
      with the center line of Arapahoe Street; thence
      southwesterly along the center line of Arapahoe
      Street to its intersection with the center line
      of a4th Street; thence northwesterly along the
      center line of 24th Street to its intersection
      with the center line of the northeast-southwest
      alley between Larimer and Lawrence Streets; thence
      southwesterly along the center line of said alley
      to its intersection with the center line of 23rd
      Street; thence northwesterly along the center line
      of 23rd Street to its intersection with the center
      line of the northeast-southwest alley between
      Market and Larimer Streets; thence southwesterly
      along the center line of said alley to its in-
      tersection with the center line of 20th Street;
      thence southeasterly along the center line of
      20th Street to the point of beginning.
      Section 2.      The Council fi·nds this ordinance is necessary
for the immediate preservation of the public health and public
safety and determines that it shall take effect immediately
upon its final passage and publication.

          Pursuant to and in accordance with a request from
    Councilman A. B. Holland, we are enclosing a bill for an
    ordinance changing the zoning classification of the area
    above generally described from B-4 to B-6. An exact
    description of the area involved is contained within the
    bill.
          At the moment, there is no district designated as
    B-6. As of this same date, we have transmitted to you
    an ordinance creating such a district. The bill creating
    the district and the bills providing for a change of
    zoning classification to B-6 should, of course, be co-
    related. Obviously, any bill providing for a change in
    zoning classification should not be enacted until after
    the enactment of a bill providing for the establishment
    of a B-6 district.
