BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
       SECTION l.      The zoning classification of the area in the City
and County of Denver described as follows or included within the
following boundaries shall be and hereby is changed from 0-1 to
I-2:
       Beginning at the intersection of the center lines of
       49th Avenue and Dahlia Street, which is the point of
       beginning; thence northerly along the center line of
       Dahlia Street to its intersection with the center line
       of 52nd Avenue; thence easterly along the center line
       of 52nd Avenue to its intersection with the center line
       of Forest Street; thence southerly along the center line
       of ForesA Street to its intersection with the centerline
       of 48th Avenue; thence westerly along the center line of
       48th Avenue to the point of beginning.
and that certain official map designated as N.E. 73 shall be and
hereby is withdrawn and repealed and a map designated as N.E. 73-A,
incorporating and reflecting the foregoing change in zoning classi-
fication but otherwise similar to and in all other respects main-
taining all matters and things shown on that map designated as
N.E. 73, shall be substituted in lieu thereof and thereafter shall
be a part of the official map.
       SECTION 2.      The Council finds this ordinance is necessary for
the immediate preservation of the public health and public safety
and determines that it shall take effect immediately upon its
final passage
