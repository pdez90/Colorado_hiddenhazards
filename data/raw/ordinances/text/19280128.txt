                     To the Honorable The Council, of the City and County of Denver,
                     Building.

                     Gentlemen:

                                  An application has been filed by Mr. Harry Moll,
                     for the erection of a residence on the Northeast corner of
                     Elm Street and 17th Avenue Parkway, on a lot having a fron-
                     tage or 60 feet on the parkway; said residence to h•ve a set
                     back from the 17th Ave. Par~ of 35 feet and from Elm       st.,r
                     15 feet, which would be 10 feet less than the set back re-
                     quired by Article 26 of the Building Code.

                               This application has been referred to the Board of
                     Adjustment for a recommendation and I am instructed by the
                     Board to advise the Honorable Council that the Board recommends
                     that this variation from 25 ft. to 15 ft. be allowed and that
                     a suitable ordinance be passed authorizing the Chief Building
                     Inspector to issue a permit for same.

                                This variation is recommended for the fol:J_owing
                     reasons to-wit: The lots in the half block directly North ot
                     applicants premises front on 18th Avenue a.nd under the terms
                     of the present zoning ordinance a residence could/be erected
                     thereon which would front on 18th Avenue and hav~ a side yard
                     of three feet on the Elm Street side, and the erection of Mr.
                     Moll's residence would have a 15 foot set back from the Elm St.
                     side and would not project beyond aey residence to the North as
                     these residences will face 18th Avenue. The applicants lot is
                     of a limited width, being 60 feet •ide and he is unable to ob-
                     tain any additional frontage as the premises adjacent to the
                     East is improved with a residence and in order to get a suitable
                     residence on this lot which would be of the same character as
                     the other neighboring residences, it would be necessary to make
                     a variation in the Elm Street set back.

                               As there are a considerable number of cases involving
                     adjustments in the set back regulations along the different park-
                     ways coming up from time to time, this Board would recommend to
                     the Council that a suitable ordinance be passed, if in the opinion
                     of the City Attorneys such an ordinance would be legal, granting
