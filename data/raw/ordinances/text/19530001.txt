                   FOJl AN ORDINANCE PERTAINING TO
                   EX'BPTIONS TO HEIGHT REGULATIONS
                   OF BUILDINGS AND AMENDING SECTION
                   613.1-5 OF THE REVISED MUNICIPAL
                   CODE, AS AMENDED, RELATED THERETO
                   BY ADDING A NEW SUBSECTION TO BE
                   DESIGNATED 613.1-5(4).

BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
                                                            '
      Section 1. That Section 613.1-5 of the Revised Municipal
Code, as amended, relating to exceptions to height regulations
of buildings is hereby amended by adding thereto the following:
      "613.1-5(4) Buildings may be erected in Commercial
      . "C" districts to heights in excess of the respective
        height limits herein provided, without being set
        back as provided in Section 613.1-5(3), if those
        portions which are higher than such limits cover
        not more than twenty-five per cent (25%) of the
        lot area. In computing lot areas under this sub-
        section, there shall be included all the area in
        common ownership related to any building, including
        all open spaces and other area within the property
        lines used· in connection with said building and not
        limited to those open spaces required by this Chapter.
        In any group of architecturally integrated buildings,
        all of which are in common ownership, portions of
       one or more of such buildings may exceed such
        height limitations, without being set back, pro-
        vided that the total area of the portions exceeding
        the height limitations does not cover more than
        twenty-five per cent (25%) of the total lot area
       of the entire group as computed in the manner set
        forth in the preceeding sentence.
      "Any building permit issued for the erection of a
       building under this section shall contain a legal
       description of the lot area involved as herein
       defined and a legal description of that portion or
       portions of the lot where the building will exceed
       the height limitations set forth in Section 613.1-3,
       and a copy thereof shall be recorded, at the owner~s
       expense, in the Clerk and Recorder~s office by the
       Chief Building Inspector forthwith after issuance.
       Any such permit shall contain a recital that it is
       issued pursuant to and subject to provisions of
       this section."
      Section 2.    In the opinion of the council this ordinance
is necessary for the immediate protection and preservation of
the public health, safety, convenience and general welfare,

    SUBJECT:     Height Restrictions in Commercial "C" Districts
