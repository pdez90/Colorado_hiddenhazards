      SUBJECT:      Repeal of Artiele 710 of the
                    Revised Municipal Code.


           This is a bill for an ordinance for the repeal of Article 710
      of the code, prohibiting location, eonstruetion, and operations of
      institutions for receiving, sheltering, earing for, housing, giving
      a home to, or making a home for persons of any particular kind, class
      or description, whether determined by infancy, age, nonage, sex, race,
      color, occupation, trade, profession, religion, or financial, physical,
      or mental condition, without the consent of the Council.
           This matter was discussed at the Mayor-Council Meeting held
      April 27, 1954, and is filed at your direction.
