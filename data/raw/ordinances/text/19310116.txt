                                                    August 17, 1931



             Board of Councilmen
             City and County of Denver
             City Hall
             Denver, Colorado
             Gentlemen:
             During the years 1926-7-8 at different intervals, damage
             was done and loss was sustained on property along 17th
             Avenue and along Jackson and Harll"'~~on Streets, as the
             result of storm sewers having been      eonnected with
             sanitary sewers and water backing up through the sanitary
             sewers into the basements of numerous residents.
             A test suit was brought by one of the residents and the
             case was taken to the Supreme Court, the decision on·
             which by the Supreme Court, that the City was liable.
             (Mason vs. City andCotmty of Denver, 295 Pacific, page
             788). On a basis of that suit settlements were made
             with practically all of-the persons whose residences
             were thus affected. It appears however that two of
             said residents, Charles E. Friend and G. E. Sheldon,
             did not join in the original litigation and settlements
             were not made with them. These two parties have lately
             filed claims, Mr. Friend's original claim being for the
             sum of $721.00 and Mr. Sheldon's for $1005.00. This
             office has had the Chief Building Inspector make an
             examination of the premises and the Chief Building
             Inspector places damages to the property of Mr. Friend,
             at $622.00 and to Mr. Sheldon's property at $1057.00 •
            .We have negotiated an agreement whereby Mr. Friend has
             agreed to settle in full for $575.00 and.Mr. Sheldon
             for $900.00. Ordinances to this effect have been pre-
             pared by this office.
