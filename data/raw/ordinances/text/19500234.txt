MEMORANDUM
TO:                        es, President of City Council
FROM:         Abe          an, Acting City Attorney
SUBJECT:      A ill for an Ordinance to Am.end the Zoning
              Ordinance in Respect to Signs
Section 1 of this ordinance would prohibit ground signs and
roof signs from Business t1A 11 zones and permit only those signs
in Business 11 A11 zones which.advertise a business conducted
upon the premises. Section 2 will harmonize the language of
the accessory uses in residence districts of the Sign Code
and the zoning ordinance. Section 3 provides that signs shall
observe the same setback requirements as are prescribed for
other structures, making exception for only projecting pole
signs like filling station corner signs.
The heart of this proposed bill is Section 1, and although
the sign companies have not complained of its language they
are busily engaged in erecting signs in locations prevented
by this bill. It is therefore recommended that the bill be
passed independent of and prior to the Sign Code.
This matter was discussed at the Mayor-Council meeting on
Wednesday, August 2, 1950.
