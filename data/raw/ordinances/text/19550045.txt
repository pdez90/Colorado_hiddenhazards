BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
     SECTION 1.     The following sections of Article 612. (District
Regulations) of Chapter 610. (Zoning) of the Revised Municipal
Code, as amended, are hereby amended and repealed as follows:
      (a)   Without in any way amending or changing the numbers and
letters by which the sections are designated and distinguished,
Sections .1-3(5)., 2-3(5)., .3-3(5). and .4-3(6). are hereby
amended and re-enacted as amended to read as follows:
     "M;i.nimum Si~ o_f....Q.welling Unii..     Each single unit dwelling and
each of the following dwelling units shall contain a gross floor
area of not less than six hundred square feet:            (1) Any dwelling
unit having an individual outside entrance at ground level; (2)
Any dwelling unit the floor of which dwelling unit is at ground
level or is below ground level or is less than ten feet above
ground level."
     (b)    Section .5-3(6).     is hereby repealed.
     SECTION 2.    This ordinance is hereby ordered published in
pamphlet form in accordance with the requirements of the Charter.
Publication of this ordinance shall be complete the day onVlhich
two such pahphlet forms are deposited with the City Clerk.             As soon
as practicable thereafter, the City Clerk shall cause to be publish-
ed in the official newspaper a legal notice declaring publication
complete.
