                     Gentlemen:
                     The Board of Adjustment at a regular meeting held on
                     Tuesday March 2nd 1943 took the following action
                     relative to a proposed change in the zone map at West
                     6th Avenue and Osceola and Perry Str~ets.
                     The following motion was adopted by unanimous vote ot
                     the Board:
                               "That the Board recommend to the Council
                                that the present Business "A" Zone on
                                Osceola Street between West 5th Avenue
                                and West 6th Avenue be changed to a Residence
                                "A" Zone and that the northeast and northwest
                                corner of West 6th Avenue and Perry Street
                                be changed from a Residence "A" Zone to a
                                Business "A" Zone."    ·
                     Attached herewith is a bill for an ordinance to amend the
                     zone map and make the changes as recommended.
