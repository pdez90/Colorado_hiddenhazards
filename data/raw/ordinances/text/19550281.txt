      We are enclosing a Bill for an Ordinance relating
to zoning, amending the provisions of the zoning ordin-
ance as they relate to accessory uses in all residential
districts. The effect of this ordinance is to remove
all limitations on the gross floor area in detached
garages and detached carports in the residential dis-
tricts.
      This Bill is submitted as recommended by your zoning
committee and in accordance with the discussion of July
27, 1955.
