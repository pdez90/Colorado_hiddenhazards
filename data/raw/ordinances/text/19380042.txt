                                  Re:    Bill for an ordinance relating to
                                         transportation of flammable liquids.
              Gentlemen:
              The attached bill for an ordinance, prepared by this office,
              results from conferences extending over a considerable period
              of time, which conferences were attended by a committee of men
              engaged in the sale of petroleum products in the City and
              County of Denver and also by Chief Boyne, of the Fire Preven-
              tion Bureau. The attached bill is submitted for the considera-
              tion of your august body, being introduced by Councilman
              Rosenthal by request.
              It provides, in substance, that no deliveries of gasoline to
              retail filling stations shall be made by any vehicles with a
              capacity in excess of 1500 gallons; provides for a routing of
              transport vehicles, and further the said transport vehicles
              should deliver to bulk stations only; provides for an inspection
              by the Manager of Safety and Excise of said equipment; contains
              several regulatory provisions concerning equipment of both trans-
