        Gentlemen:

        Attached herewith is a Bill for an ordinance authorizing and directing
        the Chief Building Inspector of the City and County of Denver to issue
        a building perm.it to Mr. Robe A. Wilson pursuant to Ordinance 101, Series
        of 1928 for the erection of three single family residences on a plot of
        ground 260 feet by 80 feet located on the south side of Montview Boulevard
        between Jasmine Street and Kearney Street, said residences to set back from
        the south line of Montview Boulevard a distance of 25 feet; the east line
        of the residence adjacent to K~ameria Street to set back from the west
        line of said street in line with the residence located south of applicant's
        premises between said premises and East 19th Avenue; the west line of the
        residence adjacent to Jasmine Street to set back from said street in line
        with the residence located south of applicant's premises between said
        premises and East 19th Avenue.

        The Board of Adjustment would recommend to the Council that tbis orainance
        be passed for the reason tr~t applicant's lots are of such limited depth
        (80'} that in order to get residences of ordinary size on these lots a var-
        iance in the set back from Montview Boulevard from 35 feet to 25 feet be
        granted.

        Directly east of applicant's premises and between Kearney Street and Krameria
        Street there is now a residence facing north on M.ontview Boulevard with a
        set back of 25 feet.
