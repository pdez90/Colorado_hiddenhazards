          Gentlemen:

          Concerning Councilman's Bill No. 85, Series of 1949, upon which Bill
          a public hearing will be held on May 16, 1949, I will not be able to
          appear at said hearing because of a three-week tour of duty with the
          u. S. Army.
          I represent some of the proponents of the Bill. I merely want to
          make the following observations concerning the reasons why this Bill
          should receive your favorable consideration and action:

                 1.   Lincoln Street from East Fourth Avenue to East Twentieth
                      Avenue, with the exception of a part of the four one-half
                      Blocks concerned, is zoned for business.

                 2.   The four one-half Blocks concerned are practically sur-
                      rounded on three sides by business establishments of all
                      types.

                 3.   Within the area of the four one-half Blocks and facing on
                      Lincoln Street, there is now a garage, drug store and other
                      commercial establishments and businesses.

                 4. Certainly no other single residences would ever be built
                      or constructed within the area affected.

                 5. A considerable portion of the area involved is unimproved
                      and rezoning to a Commercial 11 B" District would immediately
                      permit the making of substantial improvements; put the
                      property to a high beneficial use; permit the owners to
                      realize some return from the improvements; and be a further
                      source of tax revenue for the City.
