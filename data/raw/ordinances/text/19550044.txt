                FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO zrnJING;
                AW.ENDING CERTAIN SECTIONS RELATING TO
                SIDE SETBACKS CONTAINED IN ARTICLE
                612. (DISTRICT REGULATIONS) OF CHAP-
                TER 610. (ZONING) OF THE REVISED
                MUNICIPAL CODE, AS AMENDED
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
       SECTION 1.   The following sections of Article 612. (District
Regulations) of Chapter 610. (Zoning) of the Revised Municipal
Code, as amended, are hereby amended as follows:
       (a)   Without in any way amending or changing the numbers and
letters by which the sections are designated and distinguished,
Sections .l-3(2)(c)., .2-3(2)(c). and .3-3(2)(c). are hereby
amended and re-enacted as amended to read as £ollows:
       "Side Setback.    On Zone Lots less than thirty feet in width,
all structures shall be set in a distance of not less than three
feet from each side line of the Zone Lot.             On Zone Lots thirty or
more feet in width, all structures shall be set in not less than
the following distances from each side line of the Zone Lot:
(1)   Any structure designed or used, either wholly or partly, for
residential occupancy - five feet; (2) Electric substation, gas
regulator station - five feet; ( 3) Any structure containing any
other Use by Right      - ten feet;   ( 4) Any structure containing an

Accessory Use    - five feet.     Regardless of the width of the Zone
Lot, any accessory structure which is located as far to the rear
of the Zone Lot as permitted hereunder may be located within not
less than two feet from each side line of the Zone Lot."

         SECTION 2.      This ordinance is hereby ordered published in
    pamphlet form in accordance with the requirements of the
    Charter.      Publication of this ordinance shall be complete the
    day on which two such pamphlet forms are deposited with the City
    Clerk.      As soon as practicable thereafter, the City Clerk shall
    cause to be published in the official newspaper a legal notice
    declaring publication complete.

    safety, and determines that it shall take effect immediately
