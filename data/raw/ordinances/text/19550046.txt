                  FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING;
                  AMENDING CERTAIN SECTIONS RELATING TO

                  OF CHAPTER 610. (ZONING) OF THE REVISED
                  MUNICIPAL CODE, AS AMENDED.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
      SECTION 1.         The following sections of Article 612. (District
Regulations) of Chapter 610. (Zoning) of the Revised Municipal
Code, as amended, are hereby amended as follows:
      (a)      Section .l-3(l)(c).      Unobstructed Open Space on Zone
Lot, is hereby amended and re-enacted as amended to read as
follows:
      11
           .l-3(l)(c).    Unobstructed Open Space on Zone Lot.     On the
same Zone Lot       for each structure designed or used, either wholly
or partly, for residential occupancy there shall be provided at
least 3,000 square feet of Unobstructed Open Space which shall be
unobstructed except by landscaping fixtures or permitted fences."
      (b)     Section .2-3(l)(c).       Unobstructed Open Space on Zone
Lot, is hereby amended and re-enacted as amended to read as
follows:
      ".2-3(l)(c).        Unobstructed Ooen Soace on Zone Lot.     On the
same Zone Lot for each structure designed or used, eithr wholly
or partly, for residential occupancy or as a pre-school there shall
be provided at least 3,000 square feet of Unobstructed Open Space
which shall be unobstructed except by landscaping fixtures or
permitted fences."

Charter.   Publication of this ordinance shall be complete the

City Clerk.      As soon as practicable thereafter, the City Clerk
shall cause to be published in the official newspaper a legal
notice declaring publication complete.
