                                                                                              FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING,
                                                                                              AMENDING THE OFFICIAL MAP AND CHANG-
                                                                                              ING THE ZONING CLASSIFICATION FOR A
                                                                                              CERTAIN DESCRIBED AREA.
                  BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
                                         SECTION 1.                                                         The zoning classification of the area in the
                  City and County of Denver described as follows or included
                  within the following boundaries, said described area being a
                  part of the area shown on that certain official map designated
                  as N.W. 18, shall be and hereby is changed from R-3 to P-1:
                                     Lots l to 4 inclusive, South 93 1 of Lots 5 and
                                     6, Lots 10 to 14 inclusive, Block 31, Hager's
                                     Addition to Highland.
                  and that certain official map designated as N.W. 18 shall be
                  and hereby is withdrawn and repealed and a map designated as
                  N.1111. 18-A, incorporating and reflecting the foregoing change

                  in zoning classification, shall be substituted in lieu thereof
                  and thereafter shall be a part of the official map.
                                     SECTION 2.                                                            The Council finds this ordinance is necessary
                  for the immediate preservation of the public health and public
                  safety and determines that it shall take effect immediately
                  upon its final passage and publication.
