          Section 15.   OPERATORS AND EMPLOYEES.     No operator
of or employee in any parking lot licensed hereunder shall com-
mence working therein until he shall have presented himself to
the Police Department for investigation and shall have secured
a certificate from the Auto Theft Bureau stating that he has no
previous criminal record according to the files of the      Police
Department.   Such operator or employee shall also have at all
times during his operation of or employment in a parking lot
licensed hereunder, an operator's license issued under the laws
of the State of Colorado concerning drivers of automobiles.
          Section 16.   PENALTY.   Any person owning, operating

or employed in a parking lot licensed under the provisions of
this ordinance, who shall violate any of the provisions hereof,
shall upon conviction be punished by a fine not exceeding three
hundred dollars ($300.00) or imprisonment for a period not ex-
ceeding ninety (90) days, or by both such fine and imprisonment.
          Section 17.   EMERGENCY CLAUSE.     In the opinion of the
Council this ordinance is necessary for the immediate protec-
tion and preservation of the public health, safety, conven-
ience and welfare, and it is enacted for that purpose and shall
be in full force and effect immediately after its passage and
final publication.
