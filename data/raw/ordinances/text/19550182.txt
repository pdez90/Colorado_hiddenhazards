             FOR AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO ZONING,
             AMENDING THE OFFICIAL MAP AND CHANG-
             ING TrE ZONING CLASSIFICATION FOR A
             CERTAIN DESCRIBED AREA.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
     SECTION 1.   The zoning classification of the area in the
City and County of Denver described as follows or included with-
in the following boundaries, said described area being a part
of the area shown on that certain official map designated as
s.w. 24, shall be and hereby is changed from R-2 to B-4:
     Lots 36 to 44 inclusive, Block 4, West Lawn Gardens.
and the zoning classification of the area in the City and County
of Denver described as follows or included within the following
boundaries, said described area being a part of the area shown on
that certain official map designated as s.w. 24, shall be and here-
                         ~,-..!> 41- o./.
by is changed from R-2/to P-1:
     Lots 45 to 56 inclusive, Block 4, West Lawn Gardens;
     Lots 15 to 25 inclusive, Block 3, West Lawn Gardens.
and that certain official map designated as s.w. 24 shall be and
hereby is withdrawn and repealed and a map designated as s.w. 24-A,
incorporating and reflecting the foregoing changes in zoning classi-
fication, shall be substituted in lieu thereof and thereafter
shall be a part of the official map.
     SECTION 2.   The Council finds this ordinance is necess_ary
for the immediate preservation of the public health and public
safety and determines that it. shall take effect immediately upon
its final passage and publication.
