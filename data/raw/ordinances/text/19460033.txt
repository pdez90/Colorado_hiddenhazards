                  FOR AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE PLAT OF
                  CHAFFEE PARK, HllIGHTS, IN THE CITY AND
                  COUNTY OF DENV'ER.


     BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY AND COUNTY OF DENVER:
     WHEREAS, the owner of the following described parcel of land in the
City and County of Denver, State of Colorado, to-wit:
               All of the East One-half (Et) of the Southwest
               One-quarter (SW¼) of Section Sixteen (16),
               Township Three South (T. 3 s.}, Range Sixty-Eight
               West (R. 68 W.) of the Sixth Principal Meridian,
               in the City and County of Denver, State of Colorado,
               excepting those certain parts of the streets now
               designated as West Fit'ty-second (W. 52nd) Avenue
               and Tejon Street which have heretofore been conveyed
               to the City and County of Denver,
has submitted to the Council a plat of a proposed subdivision of said
land which shows the streets, alleys,"Public Park", and the topography
of the platted territory; and
    WHEREAS, said plat is accompanied by the proper certificates in
accordance with the requirements of the Charter and Ordinances of said City
and County as shown upon said plat so submitted.
    NOW, THEREFORE,
    Section 1: That said Plat of Chaffee Park Heights, in the City and
County of Denver, Sate of Colorado, be, and the same is hereby approved
and the streets, alleys and "Public Park", shown on said Plat, are hereby
accepted by the City and County of Denver.
